E-WASTE EDUCATION

Know what to do before you throw it away.

Simple guidance for safer and more responsible handling of electronic waste.

01

Reuse before recycling

If a device still works, consider repairing, reusing or donating it before sending it for recycling.

02

Protect your data

Back up important information and securely remove personal data from phones, computers and storage devices.

03

Do not burn electronics

Burning electronic components can release harmful substances. Use appropriate collection and recycling channels.

04

Separate batteries

Where appropriate, keep batteries and battery-powered equipment separate and follow local disposal guidance.
