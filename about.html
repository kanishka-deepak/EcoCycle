ABOUT ECYCLE

Technology with an environmental purpose.

EcoCycle is a student-focused e-waste management platform designed to make responsible recycling easier, clearer and more engaging.

OUR MISSION

Make responsible e-waste disposal accessible to everyone.

We connect people with convenient recycling options while spreading awareness about the environmental impact of discarded electronics.

Our platform brings pickup scheduling, collection information, education and impact tracking into one simple experience.

🌍

A cleaner digital future

Better disposal habits can help protect natural resources and reduce the environmental risks associated with e-waste.

OUR VALUES

What EcoCycle stands for

🌱

Sustainability

Encouraging responsible consumption, reuse and recycling.

🤝

Accessibility

Making useful recycling information easy to understand and use.

💡

Awareness

Helping communities understand why proper e-waste handling matters.
